CaImAn: Calcium and Voltage Imaging Analysis

NeuTorch: Neuron Boundary Segmentation

Extended NeuTorch with a supervised IsoRSUNet architecture for electron-microscopy connectome segmentation. Evaluated the model on 2.1 billion annotated voxels from three wasp specimens, reducing Rand-based split error by up to 52% relative to baseline.

Medical Masked Autoencoder Knowledge Distillation

Implemented PyTorch knowledge-distillation pipelines to compress masked autoencoders into tiny architectures for medical image analysis. Joint course work included training, fine-tuning, and evaluation workflows.
