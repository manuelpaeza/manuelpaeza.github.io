\begin{equation} x_{t+1} = x_t - \eta\nabla f(x_t). \label{eq:update} \end{equation}

Definition 1 (Example). A point \(x\) is stationary if \(\nabla f(x)=0\).

Theorem 1 (Formatting example). For \(f(x)=\frac{1}{2}\lVert x\rVert_2^2\), the unique minimizer is \(0\).

Proof. The squared norm is nonnegative and vanishes only at zero. ∎

Lemma 1 (Example). For this quadratic, \(\nabla f(x)=x\).

Proposition 1 (Example). With \(\eta=1\), the update in \(\eqref{eq:update}\) reaches zero in one step.
