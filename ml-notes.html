ML Research Notes

This page contains a collection of mathematical notes, training reports, and research investigations into the foundations of modern deep learning, with an emphasis on language model pretraining, optimization and learning dynamics, model architectures, and generalization.

I am particularly interested in understanding these topics through the lens of algorithms, high-dimensional probability, random optimization, and statistical inference, combining mathematical analysis with controlled empirical experiments.

A collection of empirical investigations into language model training, including training configurations, optimization experiments, architectural ablations, scaling behavior, and reproducible results.
