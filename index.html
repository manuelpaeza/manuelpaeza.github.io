Introduction

Hello! I am a final-year M.S. student in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Alexandr Andoni and Mitya Chklovskii. My research lies at the intersection of theoretical computer science, probability, machine learning, and statistics. I am particularly interested in high-dimensional randomized optimization, Markov chains, and algorithms for sampling and inference in complex probabilistic systems. More broadly, I study the computational and geometric barriers that arise in high-dimensional random systems. Previously, I earned my B.A. from Columbia University in Computer Science and Mathematics.
