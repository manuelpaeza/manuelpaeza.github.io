Introduction

Hello! I am a final-year M.S. student in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I am advised by Alexandr Andoni and Dmitri “Mitya” Chklovskii. I previously earned B.A. degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics from Columbia University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of theoretical computer science, high-dimensional probability, statistical mechanics, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in high-dimensional randomized optimization, Markov chain mixing, and algorithms for sampling and inference in complex probabilistic models. More broadly, I seek to understand how the geometric and probabilistic structure of high-dimensional systems shapes computational complexity and the limits of efficient algorithms.
